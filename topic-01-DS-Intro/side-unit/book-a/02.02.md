### Descriptive statistics for numerical data

The following explains each descriptive statistic, at the end there are exercises.

#### Measures of Location

Measures of location provide estimates of a single value that in some fashion represents "centering" of the entire set of data.

##### Mean
The most common is the average or formally called the arithmetic mean.

The mean is the sum of all observations, divided by the number of observations.

For the [Facebook survey data](./archives/facebook-survey.xlsx), the sum of the data in column D for hours online/week is 206. by dividing this by the number of students (33), we compute the mean as:

~~~
206/33 = 6.24
~~~

You can also calculate the mean in Excel by using the AVERAGE(data range) function.
For the Facebook data it would be AVERAGE(E4:E36) = 176.97 if we were looking for the mean number of facebook friends.

The mean is unique for every data set and is meaningful for both interval and ratio data.
**However it can be affected by outliers, observations that are radically different from the rest.**

---

##### Median
Another measure is the median, the middle value when the data are arranged from smallest to largest. For an odd number of observations, the median is the middle of the sorted numbers. For an even number of observations, the median is the mean of the two middle numbers.

To find the median for the number of friends in the facebook data in Excel we have a couple of options, we can sort the values in the Facebook data (from A-Z or smallest to largest) and then determine the median. Since we have 33 observations the median will be the 17th observation which in this case is  120 Friends, see below:

![](./img/08.png)

The excel function MEDIAN(data range) would also provide the result. In this case it would be:

~~~
=median(E4:E36)
~~~


The median is meaningful for ratio, interval and ordinal data.

*Unlike the mean, the median is not affected by outliers. *

---

##### Mode
The mode is the observation that occurs most frequently. The mode can easily be identified from counting the occurances of each value. For example in the Facebook data we can see that there are 6 occurances of hours online/week = 4. This means the mode for the variable hours online/per week is 4 hours. This the most common answer given by the participants.

![](./img/09.png)

You can also use the Excel function MODE(data range).

~~~
= mode(D4:D36)
~~~
